HouseTap Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

HouseTap is an Android app that shows and controls your Home Assistant smart-home server on a tablet or TV. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: HouseTap talks to your own Home Assistant server, collects nothing about you, and has no accounts, analytics, tracking or ads.

Information the app handles

  • Your Home Assistant address and sign-in. When you connect HouseTap, it stores your server address and the sign-in token Home Assistant gives it. These stay on your device, and the token is stored encrypted. They are sent only to your own Home Assistant server.
  • Your smart-home information. To draw the dashboard, HouseTap reads information from your Home Assistant, such as room names, device states, temperatures, calendar events and camera pictures, and sends your commands back to it. This information is shown on screen and kept only on the device. HouseTap does not send it anywhere else.
  • Dashboard settings. Your layout, theme and other preferences are saved on the device.
  • Local network discovery. During setup, HouseTap looks for Home Assistant on your Wi-Fi network. This happens only on your local network.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer of HouseTap does not run any server that receives your data. The app has no user accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and does not sell or share personal information.

Third parties the app may contact

  • Google Play or the Amazon Appstore, to process the optional one-time Pro purchase and to check that you own it. Payment details are handled by the store, never by HouseTap. See Google's privacy policy or Amazon's privacy notice.
  • Album art servers. If you use the music card with a service such as Spotify through Home Assistant, the app downloads the cover picture for what's playing directly from that service's image server. Like any web request, this shares your device's IP address with that server.
  • Crash reports. If you allow it in your device settings, Google Play or Amazon may send the developer anonymous crash and performance reports. These do not include your Home Assistant data.

Feedback you choose to send

If you use "Send feedback", HouseTap opens a pre-filled GitHub issue or email for you to review and send. It includes technical details such as the app version, device model and Android version. It never includes your Home Assistant address, sign-in token or device names. Anything you send is handled under GitHub's or your email provider's privacy policy.

Removing your data

Everything HouseTap stores is on your device. Signing out in Settings removes your sign-in, and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes everything else. You can also revoke HouseTap's access at any time in Home Assistant under your profile's Security settings.

Children

HouseTap is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
